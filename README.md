👑 BGR eSports

Ultimate Professional Esports Tournament Platform

Play. Compete. Dominate.

BGR eSports is a professional esports tournament management platform designed for players, tournament organizers and platform administrators.

Our goal is to provide a secure, scalable, high-performance tournament ecosystem with a separate Owner Website and Player Progressive Web App (PWA).
